# Rowkery website (Next.js)

**Dark** (2026-10-14): one black sheet, white headlines, grey copy, pill buttons, the real dark app screens inside rounded panels, and the penguin as mascot. Dark only: there is no light theme and no Appearance toggle.

- **Look:** the tokens and every rule are in `app/globals.css`, ported value for value from the approved page. White pills are Download; grey pills are everything else. Outside the screens, colour only means something: green a read, red a refusal or prd, orange the masked part of a host.
- **Story** (2026-10-16: agent first, then security, then ⌘K): Meet Rowkery (the build, the name turning from Rookery into Rowkery, one line, Download and View on GitHub, a switch between With your AI agent, a scene of your agent, Rowkery and your database, and On your own, the app window); *Let your AI agent read your database / while you keep full control* (read-only on your Mac, nothing changes until you press Commit, sensitive columns, the log, the checkpoint, the tables it can see, timed sharing, the masked host); *Security first*; *Built for the keyboard*; *See why a query is slow / and what's holding it up* (⌘E plans, safe mode per connection, Sessions); *Quick to open / and quick to query*; *What we're exploring* (a band of databases we'd like to add, no dates, a link to GitHub Discussions); and the close, *Try Rowkery*: Download, the unquarantine command, Build from source, a sponsor note from Lucas, and the footer. Long headings break into lines (`.ln`) on wide screens instead of using commas.
- **Screens:** real app screens rendered from the app in Chromium, as `-dark.webp` at 2x in `assets/img`, imported with their size in `lib/shots.ts` and drawn by `Shot.tsx`. Fragments fade out at the card's edge. They are imported rather than served from `public/`, so a build only ships the screens a page on it draws.
- **Motion:** CSS only. The hero rises in once at load and the name turns from Rookery into Rowkery (`Hero.tsx`); three moments loop over the window every 12 s, yours or the agent's, the switch turning between them until someone clicks it (`HeroShot.tsx`); every card plays a short loop (`CommitDemo.tsx`, `MaskDemo.tsx`, `CardMotion.tsx`: ⌘K typing, Quick Look, the log, tables, sharing, the host), paused while its card is off screen (`Reveal.tsx`); the databases band slides on a 50 s loop (`Exploring.tsx`); on the checkpoint four calls ride the rail every 12 s, reads passing Rowkery's gate to the database and coming back as rows, refusals stamped and sent back (`Checkpoint.tsx`). Blocks marked `.reveal` rise in once as they come into view (`Reveal.tsx`, an IntersectionObserver), and the speed bars wait at zero until their card does. The layout's boot script marks the page `.js` before first paint; without scripts, or if the bundle hasn't come alive within 2.5 s, everything simply shows. `prefers-reduced-motion` stills everything: one toast, two calls held either side of the gate, the bars at their timings.
- **Narrow screens:** the rules under 860 px are the approved page's, plus three the 1440 drawing didn't need: the checkpoint's end stations sit on the card's padding line and its calls stay a third of the stage wide; the masked host's READ-ONLY tag moves under it on a phone; the longest stamps shrink a little below 375 px.
- **Copy facts:** one-click setup is only for Claude Desktop and Claude Code; any other MCP client runs `Rowkery --mcp`. No offline claims (the app checks for updates): say the agent's connection runs on your Mac with no server of ours in between. No millisecond figures on the page until the known window-switch slowdown is fixed. No signing promises: the Mac app isn't signed yet, and sponsors cover the developer fee.
- **Fonts:** IBM Plex Sans (400, 500) and IBM Plex Mono (400, 500) (SIL OFL), self-hosted from `app/fonts` through `next/font/local`, which preloads every face it lists.
- **Stack:** Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), static export, plain CSS, no animation library.

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:3000 with hot reload
npm run build      # static site in out/
```

Or `make web` from the repo root (build + serve `out/` on :3000).

**Deploy:** Vercel (or Cloudflare Pages), root directory `apps/web`, build command `npm run build`, output directory `out`.

**Placeholders:** `DOWNLOAD_URL` and `RELEASE` in `lib/site.ts`. Every Download button links to `DOWNLOAD_URL`, and while it is empty they scroll to the closing section. `RELEASE` is the build named in the hero's pill and under the closing buttons.

**Check** (after `npm run build`; it needs Playwright):

- `node scripts/resolution-check.mjs [dir]` photographs every section at eight window sizes (360 to 2560 wide) and reports sideways scroll, images that didn't load, text cut off or broken onto two lines, anything leaving its card (the checkpoint is checked through a whole loop), loops that aren't running, blocks not held back below the fold or never rising in, and speed bars that grow too early or to the wrong length. Then Reduce Motion, no scripts, a bundle that never loads, and the 404. Fails on any page error. One contact sheet per size.
- `node scripts/marketing-refresh-proof.mjs [dir]` proves the 2026-10-16 refresh at 1440x900 and 390x844: Rookery turning into Rowkery, the hero switch (turning by itself, held by a click), you before the agent on the page, Commit changing the data only after the press, the robot and masked values through a whole loop, Security first, the databases band and the sponsor note. Writes frames of each.
- `node scripts/open-source-proof.mjs [dir]` checks the open-source page at 1440x900 and 390x844: the USPs in order, no millisecond figures, the Exploring, Build from source, Sponsor and GitHub links, the closing section's order, the copy button, and headings without full stops.
- `node scripts/scroll-profile.mjs` scrolls the page top to foot in Chromium with the CPU slowed 4x and reports frame gaps and the main thread's style, layout and paint work, scrolling and sitting still. It fails if the page lays out or paints while it sits still: every looping animation moves only `transform`, `translate`, `scale` or `opacity`, so the compositor plays it and scrolling never waits on it (an animated `left`, `width`, `background-position` or colour repaints every frame for as long as the page is open).

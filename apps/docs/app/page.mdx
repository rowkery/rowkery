# Rowkery

<p className="lead">Rowkery is a Mac app for Postgres and SQLite. You can browse and edit your data yourself, or let an AI agent read it through the server built into the app. Only you can change it.</p>

## The window

Each window is one connection and one database. Open as many as you like; they sit as pills in the title bar, and <kbd>⌘1</kbd>–<kbd>⌘9</kbd> switch between them.

- **Schema column** on the left: the database, its host and environment, then your tables and views. <kbd>⌘B</kbd> hides it.
- **Tabs** in the middle: query tabs for SQL you write, table tabs for browsing a table.
- **Inspector** on the right: Quick Look, Review SQL or New connection, beside your data and never over it.

## What it needs

- macOS on Apple Silicon or Intel. Linux and Windows build from source, but they aren't tested the way the Mac app is.
- Nothing else. The Postgres and SQLite drivers are part of the app, and there's no account to make.
